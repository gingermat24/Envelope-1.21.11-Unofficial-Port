**Seal stamp** is an item that allows sealing [letters](@envelope:letter) and [packages](@envelope:package), adding a proof of authorship and integrity.

## Obtaining
### Crafting
Seal stamps can be crafted in a [crafting table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafting_Table). A crafted stamp has no die configured; when used, it makes an impression of the first letter or number in the sealer's name.
Combine a seal stamp with any dye in a crafting table to make a colored seal stamp. Colored stamps keep their color when configured with a die at the Mail Service.

### Mailing
Mail a seal stamp with a matching item to the <Asset location="envelope:gui/address_mail_service" width={20} /> Mail Service to configure one of its available emblem dies:
- Book → book
- Golden Apple → apple
- Axe, Hoe, Pickaxe, Shovel, or Sword → the matching tool emblem
- Skeleton Skull → skeleton

These recipes configure emblem dies, not a chosen A-Z letter. A stamp without a configured die uses the sealer's first letter or number when used.

### Loot
The storage platform on the [collapsed mail hub](@envelope:collapsed_mail_hub) can contain stamps configured with a heart, creeper, letter/envelope, crossed swords, or villager die.

## Usage
<div style={{ display: "flex", flexWrap: "wrap", width: "100%", alignItems: "flex-start" }}>
    <div style={{ flex: 1, marginTop: "-20px"}}>
        Seal stamp can be used on a [letter](@envelope:letter) or [package](@envelope:package) by picking it up in inventory
        and right-clicking with it on the target item, similar to a [bundle](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bundle).

        Seal is shown in the sealed item tooltip, along with the name of a person who sealed it.
        A colored seal stamp can also be used on an already sealed item to change the seal's color without changing its impression or signature.

        Stamp does not have durability and can infinitely create seals.
